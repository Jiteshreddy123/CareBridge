1. Executive Summary

In high-volume public cancer hospitals and regional tertiary centers across India, the critical breakdown in treatment continuity does not usually occur inside operating rooms or chemotherapy infusion suites. Instead, it occurs after the patient is discharged.

Oncology discharge summaries and prescriptions are predominantly recorded in abbreviated, English-language medical terminology (e.g., "CBC w/ diff Day 10, CECT Thorax/Abd review, Cap Capecitabine 1000mg BID"). Patients and family caregivers often travel hundreds of kilometers from non-English-speaking regions, speaking languages such as Hindi, Telugu, Bengali, Tamil, or Kannada.

The lack of clear, culturally accessible, and structured care instructions frequently leads to missed lab investigations, violated fasting protocols before essential PET-CT scans, unaddressed chemotherapy toxicity, and ultimately, treatment drop-out. CareBridge NCG was engineered to solve this crisis by translating dense discharge notes into actionable, patient-friendly, multilingual care passes with zero cognitive friction and absolute clinical safety.

Core Mission Statement

"To ensure that no cancer patient drops out of curative or palliative therapy simply because they or their family could not understand what to do, why it mattered, or where to go next."

2. Clinical Landscape & Problem Formulation

Longitudinal cancer care encompasses multi-week cycles of systemic chemotherapy, sequential radiation therapy, diagnostic response evaluations, and palliative symptom control. The operational friction in this continuum stems from three systemic bottlenecks:

1. The Language & Health Literacy Chasm

Over 75% of patients in public healthcare setups are non-English speaking. When handed discharge papers with dense acronyms, caregivers rely on unverified advice or delayed community inquiries, creating acute anxiety and protocol violations.

2. Fragmented Out-of-Hospital Tracking

Once a patient leaves the OPD, hospital teams have zero real-time telemetry on whether prerequisite tests (e.g., ANC/WBC blood counts) are completed until the patient physically arrives—frequently resulting in cancelled chemotherapy slots.

CareBridge NCG adopts a decoupled, modern web architecture optimized for reliability, clinical data safety, and zero-barrier accessibility on low-bandwidth mobile devices.

3.2 Clinical Safety Principles (Human-in-the-Loop)

Non-Diagnostic Clinical Boundary

CareBridge is deliberately engineered with strict safety guardrails. The AI engine never formulates diagnoses or autonomously modifies clinical protocols. Every patient-facing instruction card is strictly treated as an "Unconfirmed Draft" until explicitly reviewed, customized, and validated with 1-click by the treating oncologist.

4. Core Functional Modules

Module 1: Clinician Governance Portal

Designed for oncologists and nurse navigators during high-volume OPDs. Clinicians paste or upload discharge notes. The engine decomposes the text into structured follow-up items (e.g., CBC Blood Test, Pre-Chemo Review, PET-CT Scan). Doctors verify the draft, adjust timings if necessary, and approve it into the hospital registry with a single click.

Module 2: Multilingual Patient Digital Pass

Delivered directly to patients' smartphones without requiring app store installations. Translates approved orders into 11 Indian languages (Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Kannada, etc.). Each task is rendered as a clean, standardized 4-part action card:

  • What to do: Conversational, unambiguous statement (e.g., *"Complete your blood test"*).
  • Why it matters: Contextual clinical rationale (e.g., *"Your doctor must verify white blood counts before giving chemotherapy"*).
  • Where to go: Department name, floor, room number, and check-in window.
  • Preparation Rules: Strict fasting durations, hydration, or medication pauses.
Module 3: Closed-Loop Follow-up & Dropout Prevention Queue

A real-time command dashboard for oncology navigators. Categorizes tasks into Completed, Pending, and Overdue / At-Risk. Patient risk stratification flags high-risk individuals 48 hours before chemotherapy cycles, enabling proactive outreach via phone or WhatsApp.

Module 4: Urgent Help Request & Triage Desk

Patients experiencing fever, severe nausea, or transportation issues can tap a single "Need Help" button. The escalation appears instantly on the coordinator console with triage severity ratings, preventing unnecessary emergency department admissions.

7. Conclusion & Future Roadmap

CareBridge NCG addresses one of the most pressing socio-technical challenges in modern cancer care delivery. By leveraging assistive technologies while respecting clinical hierarchies through human-in-the-loop validation, the platform transforms confusing discharge summaries into a reassuring, actionable beacon for patients and families.

Future Milestones:

  • Integration with ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) health IDs (ABHA).
  • Two-way automated WhatsApp Business API integration for offline SMS fallback.
  • Integration with hospital Electronic Medical Records (EMRs) via HL7 FHIR standards.
